About SkillBridge

SkillBridge is an intuitive career readiness platform that helps students and software candidates analyze their job description alignment, build step-by-step learning roadmaps, and practice real company interview questions.

1. Skill-Gap Analysis

Upload your resume and paste target job descriptions to calculate a transparent 0-100 readiness score and identify missing skills.

2. AI Learning Roadmap

Follow a week-by-week sequence of study milestones with direct links to documentation, tutorials, and practical exercise tasks.

3. Company Interview Prep

Practice 10 round-specific interview questions for top companies (TCS, Streebo, Google, Amazon) with an interactive AI Mock Coach.
